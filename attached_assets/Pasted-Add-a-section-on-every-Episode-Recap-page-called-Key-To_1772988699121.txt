Add a section on every Episode Recap page called:

Key Topics From This Episode

This section should list 4–6 important topics discussed in the episode, extracted from the transcript.

Each topic should be a short phrase, such as:

• Formula 1 business model
• Liberty Media acquisition
• Ferrari brand economics
• Drive to Survive impact on Formula 1
• F1 revenue model

Topics should focus on companies, strategies, industries, or concepts discussed in the episode.

Avoid generic phrases like:

• business ideas
• podcast discussion
• interesting moments

Display these topics as clickable chips or buttons.

Example layout:

Key Topics From This Episode

[ Formula 1 business model ]
[ Liberty Media acquisition ]
[ Ferrari brand economics ]
[ Drive to Survive impact ]
[ F1 revenue model ]

When a user clicks a topic, it should open the AI search within this episode, automatically asking a question about that topic.

Example behavior:

Click
Formula 1 business model

Auto-populate the AI search box with:

“What did this episode say about the Formula 1 business model?”

Then generate the answer from the transcript.

This keeps users inside the episode page and lets them quickly explore specific ideas discussed in the episode.

Goals of this feature:

• Make long episodes easier to explore
• Help users jump directly to topics they care about
• Encourage interaction with the AI search feature
• Improve navigation within the recap page

For each episode recap page, add a section called Top Questions From This Episode.

Automatically generate 5 concise questions based on the transcript that represent the most important ideas discussed in the episode.

These questions should:

• Reflect what a reader would naturally want to understand after reading the recap
• Be phrased like real Google searches
• Focus on key companies, people, strategies, or concepts discussed in the episode
• Avoid vague questions like “What did they talk about?”

Each question should expand to show a 2–3 paragraph answer generated from the transcript, with a link to jump to the relevant section in the full transcript.

Examples:

Why did Liberty Media buy Formula 1?
How does Formula 1 make money?
Why is Ferrari so valuable in Formula 1?
How did Netflix’s Drive to Survive grow Formula 1?
How much revenue does Formula 1 generate?

Below the questions include an Ask About This Episode input box where users can ask their own questions about the episode transcript.
