Add a “Top Questions About This Podcast” section inside the Ask About This Podcast tab, but keep Episode Recaps as the main focus of the page. The recap feed is the core product, so this Q&A content should support discovery and SEO without competing visually with the recaps.

Inside the Ask About This Podcast tab:
	1.	Show a short intro such as:
“Ask anything about this podcast, its themes, and past episodes.”
	2.	Below that, generate about 5 strong questions and answers about the podcast overall.
	3.	These questions should be written with SEO in mind. They should resemble things people might type into Google when trying to learn about the podcast.

Examples of the types of questions to generate:
	•	What is the Dwarkesh Podcast about?
	•	Who is Dwarkesh Patel?
	•	What topics are commonly discussed on the Dwarkesh Podcast?
	•	What are some popular episodes of the Dwarkesh Podcast?
	•	Why do people listen to the Dwarkesh Podcast?

Guidelines for the questions:
	•	Focus on search-friendly phrasing that reflects common queries about the podcast.
	•	Avoid vague questions like “What happens in this podcast?”
	•	Prefer questions about topics, themes, host background, and notable episodes.

Guidelines for the answers:
	•	Each question should have a short, clear answer (1–2 paragraphs).
	•	Answers should be generated by looking across the podcast’s episodes and transcripts, not just one episode.
	•	The goal is to summarize what the podcast typically covers and why it’s interesting.

After the 5 questions and answers, include the AI search box with text like:

“Ask your own question about this podcast.”

This keeps the flow as:
	•	Starter questions with answers (good for SEO and orientation)
	•	Then the interactive AI search for deeper exploration.

Important design goal:
	•	The Episode Recaps tab remains the default and most prominent content on the podcast page.
	•	The Q&A content lives inside Ask About This Podcast so it helps SEO and discovery but doesn’t overshadow the recap experience.
