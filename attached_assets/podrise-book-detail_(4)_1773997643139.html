About this book

Bill Perkins argues that most people optimize their entire financial lives around a goal that defeats the purpose of earning money in the first place — dying with a large number in an account. Die With Zero makes the case for converting money into experiences at the moment when you have both the resources and the physical ability to fully enjoy them. His core tool is the "net worth curve" — a model for when you should be spending versus saving based on your life stage, not a fixed retirement date.
