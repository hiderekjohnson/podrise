Build "The Daily Drop" for PodCap
What is PodCap?
PodCap is a platform for people who are obsessed with podcasts. Not podcasters — listeners. People who listen to multiple shows a week, want to discover new ones, and want to get the most out of every episode. Think of the audience as people who treat podcasts the way others treat books or news — seriously, habitually, hungrily.
What is The Daily Drop?
The Daily Drop is a daily digest that lives inside PodCap. Every day it recaps what dropped across the podcasts PodCap tracks. It's not a trade publication, it's not for the industry — it's purely for listeners. The tone is like a really smart friend who listens to everything and gives you the highlights over coffee. Informed, fun, a little witty, never stuffy. Think Morning Brew meets The Ringer. Editorial but approachable. Professional but with personality.
The goal is that a user opens it every morning and within 2-3 minutes knows exactly what dropped, what's worth their time, and maybe discovers something they didn't know they wanted to listen to.
What to mimic:

Morning Brew for tone and daily digest format
The Ringer for sports-meets-culture editorial voice
Bloomberg's The Daily Brief for clean scannable structure
A newspaper front page for visual hierarchy — there's a hero story, supporting stories, and quick hits

Build a single HTML file called the-daily-drop.html.
Structure the page in this order:

Masthead — centered at top. Says "The Daily Drop." Below it: today's date and how many episodes dropped in the last 24 hours across PodCap.
Episode of the Day — the hero. Show name above the headline, big bold episode title, 3-4 sentence write-up that tells the listener exactly why this one matters and what moment to listen for, runtime, topic tags, and a clear "Listen on PodCap →" link.
Today's Drops — a clean scannable list of 4 other episodes from the last 24 hours. One line each: show name, episode title, runtime, listen link. No long write-ups.
Stat of the Day — one surprising number from the last 24 hours. Big and prominent. One sentence of context underneath.
Quote of the Day — one pull quote from a recent episode. Attribution with show name and a link to the episode at the exact timestamp.
Footer — date, link to all episodes on PodCap, link to the weekly edition called "The Drop."
