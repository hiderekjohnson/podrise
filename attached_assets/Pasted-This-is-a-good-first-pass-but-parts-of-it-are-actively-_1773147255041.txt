These are not real evergreen topics for Elon Musk. They make the page look messy, low-quality, and auto-generated in a bad way.

Fix this by only showing broad, reusable, existing topic pages that truly match the person.

For Elon Musk, that means things like:
	•	Artificial Intelligence
	•	Tesla
	•	SpaceX
	•	Autonomous Vehicles
	•	Robotics

	•	Social Media
	•	Politics

But only show them if those topic pages already exist.

2. Fix the quotes section, because most of these are not actually notable quotes from Elon Musk

Right now the section is called Notable Elon Musk Quotes From Podcasts, but many of the quotes are not Elon Musk quotes at all. They are just other people mentioning him.

That is misleading.

Rename and restructure this section based on the actual data.

If the person is not actually speaking in the clip, then call the section:

Notable Podcast Mentions of Elon Musk

If the clip is truly from an episode where Elon is the guest and the quote is his, then it can be labeled:

Notable Quotes From Elon Musk Podcast Appearances

Do not mix the two together under one misleading heading.

This matters because users and Google both care about precision.

3. Stop treating all mentions as equally valuable

A mention in a random political or culture episode is not as valuable as:
	•	a direct guest appearance
	•	an episode centered on the person
	•	a major discussion about the person’s companies or ideas

Add ranking logic so the best items appear first.

Priority should be:
	1.	direct guest appearances
	2.	episodes primarily about the person
	3.	substantive discussions about the person
	4.	passing mentions

Right now the page is letting weak mentions dominate. That lowers quality.

If there are zero guest appearances, use something like:

Do not make a claim the data does not support.
