	1	The Daily (The New York Times) – The gold standard for daily news, usually diving deep into one major story.
	2	Up First (NPR) – A quick 10-15 minute morning briefing on the three biggest headlines of the day.
	3	The Journal (Wall Street Journal) – Similar to The Daily, but with a heavier focus on money, business, and economics.
	4	Today, Explained (Vox) – A daily explainer show that uses a more conversational tone to break down complex news stories.
	5	NBC Nightly News with Lester Holt – The audio version of the evening television broadcast; very popular with parents.
	6	Global News Podcast (BBC) – The best option for a twice-daily summary of international news from a global perspective.
	7	CBS Mornings on the Go – A podcast version of the morning TV show, focused on a mix of hard news and lifestyle stories.
	8	Post Reports (The Washington Post) – An evening rundown released at the end of the workday to catch you up on the day's events.
	9	ABC World News This Week – A weekly "best of" summary that covers the most significant headlines from the network.
	10	The Economist Podcasts – High-level analysis of global business and political trends for a more intellectual audience.
	11	Breaking Points (Hosts: Krystal Ball & Saagar Enjeti) – The leader in this space, known for having a progressive and a conservative host challenge mainstream media narratives.
	12	The Megyn Kelly Show – Features long-form interviews and commentary that challenges traditional media perspectives.
	13	The Ben Shapiro Show (The Daily Wire) – One of the most-downloaded daily news podcasts, offering a conservative breakdown of the news.
	14	Stay Tuned with Preet (Host: Preet Bharara) – Focuses on law, justice, and politics from a former U.S. Attorney’s perspective.
	15	The Majority Report (Host: Sam Seder) – A daily progressive talk show that focuses on policy and media criticism.
	16	Puck’s The Powers That Be: Daily – A "behind the scenes" look at the power players in Wall Street, Washington, and Hollywood.
	17	Pod Save America (Crooked Media) – Hosted by former Obama staffers, it offers a liberal perspective on the day's political news.
	18	The Gist (Host: Mike Pesca) – A daily show that analyzes news and culture with a slightly satirical, independent lens.
	19	Democracy Now! – A daily, independent news hour that has a long history of focusing on social justice and international issues.
	20	Rising (The Hill) – Originally where the Breaking Points hosts started; it continues as a daily news show with a similar "heterodox" vibe.
	21	The News Agents – A very popular daily UK-based show that is rapidly growing in the US for its sharp, high-speed analysis.
	22	Redacted (Hosts: Clayton & Natali Morris) – Focuses on "under-reported" stories and challenges corporate media storylines.
	23	Citations Needed – A podcast that specifically analyzes how the media uses PR and "messaging" to influence the news.
	24	The Daily Beans – A woman-led daily news show focused on US politics and legal developments.
	25	The Young Turks – One of the largest independent progressive news networks, covering headlines with a high-energy, opinionated style.
