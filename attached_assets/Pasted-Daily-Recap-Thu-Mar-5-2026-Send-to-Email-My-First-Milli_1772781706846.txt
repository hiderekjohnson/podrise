🎧 Apple Podcasts · Spotify

TL;DR: Shaan Puri engages with Sheel Mohnot to uncover key investment strategies for the future, focusing on the implications of AI and identifying resilient business ideas. They explore the potential winners in the AI race and dissect notable investment failures.

What They Talk About
The conversation kicks off with personal lessons learned from investing, transitioning into the competitive landscape of AI and its impact on businesses. Shaan probes Sheel about what makes certain business models more resilient than others, while Sheel shares insights on how to avoid mistakes seen in past investment failures.

Key Insights:

The "last mile problem" signifies the challenge of delivering products or services efficiently in a competitive market.
Understanding the AI race helps investors identify which companies may thrive as the tech evolves.
A focus on "AI-proof" business ideas can lead to more stable investments in an unpredictable market.
Historical business blunders serve as crucial learning points for current and future investors.
"Investing is less about knowing what will succeed and more about understanding the landscape where those successes will emerge."
