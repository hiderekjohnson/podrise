We need to improve the blurb text under both the People Mentioned and Companies Mentioned sections on every episode page. The current blurbs are generic and describe who the person is or what the company does — that information is on Wikipedia. PodCap's value is what was actually said about them in this specific episode. Generic blurbs also hurt SEO because they're identical or near-identical across multiple episode pages.
The rule for both People and Companies blurbs:
Describe the specific claim, argument, or story from this episode about this person or company. Never describe who they are generically. One sentence, specific to the episode. Do not attribute to a named speaker since we don't have speaker-tagged transcripts.
The rule for handling lack of speaker attribution:
Since transcripts are not speaker-tagged, do not attribute claims to specific hosts or guests. Instead write in passive or general terms — "was cited," "was highlighted," "was referenced" — focused on what was said, not who said it.
