The goal is to surface the five most useful questions someone might search in Google about this podcast, along with concise answers.

How it should work:
	1.	Generate 5 SEO-friendly questions

Analyze all episode transcripts from the podcast and generate five questions that people are most likely to search for about this podcast or the topics it covers.

The questions should be:

• clear and specific
• phrased like real Google searches
• focused on themes, strategies, or ideas commonly discussed on the podcast

Avoid vague questions like:

• What is this podcast about?
• What do the hosts talk about?

Examples for a podcast like My First Million might look like:

• What business ideas are discussed on My First Million?
• How do Sam Parr and Shaan Puri evaluate startup ideas?
• What are common ways to build a million dollar business?
• What industries do My First Million hosts think have opportunity?
• What are the most popular business strategies discussed on My First Million?
	2.	Generate answers

Each question should have a short answer (2–3 paragraphs) summarizing what the podcast typically says about that topic based on the transcripts.

The answers should reference the themes that come up across multiple episodes.
	3.	Display format

On the AI Search page show:

Top Questions About This Podcast

Then list the questions in an accordion style list where clicking the question reveals the answer.

This section serves two purposes:
