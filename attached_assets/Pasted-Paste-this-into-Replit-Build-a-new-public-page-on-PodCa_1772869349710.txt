Also add this page to the footer as:

For Podcasters

Core tone

This page should sound like real fans of podcasts. Not a corporate FAQ. Not legal copy. Not a blog post. Not “we’re sorry.” We genuinely admire podcasters and want that to come through.

At the very top, include a line like:

WE ❤️ PODCASTERS

Near the top, include a short section that says, in substance:

Running a great podcast is incredibly hard. Showing up consistently, preparing, researching, interviewing, editing, publishing, and keeping an audience engaged takes real skill and discipline. We genuinely admire podcasters. If we had the talent to run a great podcast ourselves, we’d probably be doing that instead of building another web tool.

Then transition into why PodCap exists:

The issue is not that podcasts are bad. The issue is that there are too many great episodes and not enough time in the day. Even people who love podcasts fall behind. PodCap exists to help listeners quickly understand what an episode is about so they can decide what is worth their time.

Main message to communicate

PodCap is not replacing podcasts. It helps people discover which episodes they want to listen to.

The page should make the argument that:
	•	Many potential listeners never start long episodes because they are unsure whether the episode is worth the time
	•	A recap reduces that friction
	•	When someone reads a recap and realizes an episode is for them, that can lead to a more qualified listen
	•	More qualified listeners may lead to better engagement and completion behavior
	•	Summary pages also create more search entry points for podcasts and individual episodes
	•	Superfans still listen, and many casual fans may listen more often when they know what an episode covers

You do not need to use those exact words, but that logic should be clear.
