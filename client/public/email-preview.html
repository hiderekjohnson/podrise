THE TIM FERRISS SHOW

EPISODE
Morning Routines of Billionaires
GUEST
Tim Ferriss
Author and Investor
LENGTH
1 hr 12 min

🎧 Apple Podcasts · Spotify

TLDR

Tim interviews three billionaire founders about their non-negotiable morning habits. The consensus: protecting your first two hours from external inputs is the single biggest productivity lever. All three guests independently arrived at the same conclusion about not checking email before 10am.

What They Talk About

Tim digs into the exact first 90 minutes of each founder's day. One runs a 4B fintech and starts with a 20-minute worry journal. Another does cold plunges followed by 30 minutes of writing. The real insight is not any single habit but the underlying philosophy: treating morning hours as a sacred creative window that no one else can touch.

KEY INSIGHTS
 A 4B fintech founder starts every day with a 20-minute worry journal, rating each anxiety 1-10 on likelihood
Tim's updated morning stack: 10 min meditation, cold plunge, then 30 min writing before any device
All three billionaire guests independently decided to never check email before 10am
Morning routines are about protecting creative hours, not optimizing for productivity metrics
““ "I used to wake up and immediately reach for my phone. Now I treat my first two hours like a sacred creative window. My output tripled.""
