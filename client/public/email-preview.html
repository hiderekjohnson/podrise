My First Million — Episode #482: "The AI Gold Rush"

Sam Parr and Shaan Puri break down three massive AI business opportunities hiding in plain sight.

The guys kick off with a wild stat: enterprise AI spending hit $180B this year, but 70% of companies still have not deployed a single AI tool internally.

  • Sam reveals his "boring AI" thesis — the biggest money is in automating unsexy back-office workflows, not building chatbots
  • Shaan shares a framework for spotting AI startups that will actually survive: "Look for companies where AI is the engine, not the paint job"
  • They debate whether AI coding tools will replace junior developers or create 10x more demand for software
"The best AI businesses right now are not selling AI — they are selling the picks and shovels. Everyone wants to be OpenAI, but the real money is in being the company that helps Bob Insurance Agency actually use ChatGPT."

Key Takeaway: Focus on distribution, not technology. The companies winning the AI race are the ones with existing customer relationships, not the ones with the best models.

The Tim Ferriss Show — Episode #714: "Morning Routines of Billionaires"

Tim interviews three billionaire founders about their non-negotiable morning habits.

This episode dives deep into the first 90 minutes of the day for some of the most productive people in the world.

  • Founder #1 (unnamed, runs a $4B fintech) starts every day with a 20-minute "worry journal" — writes down every anxiety, then rates each 1-10 on actual likelihood
  • Tim shares his updated morning stack: 10 min meditation, cold plunge, then 30 min of writing before checking any device
  • All three guests agreed: the single biggest productivity hack is not checking email before 10am
"I used to wake up and immediately reach for my phone. Now I treat my first two hours like a sacred creative window. My output tripled."

Key Takeaway: Your morning routine is not about optimization — it is about protecting your most creative hours from the tyranny of other people agendas.
